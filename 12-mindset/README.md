# 12 — Mindset

> Tools and techniques make you faster. Mindset determines whether you
> stay welcome, stay sane, and keep getting better over years.

The skills in the earlier sections are mechanical — you can learn them
in an afternoon. The habits here are the ones that separate someone who
*can* contribute from someone projects are glad to have around. They're
harder because they're about your defaults under pressure, frustration,
and ego.
