# 13 — Hidden Knowledge

> The code is public. The context isn't. Most of what determines whether
> your contribution succeeds lives in places the repository never tells
> you about.

This section is the unwritten curriculum: how projects are governed, who
really decides, where the roadmap actually lives, what license terms
mean for you, and the social and legal machinery humming under every
repo. None of it is in the README. All of it shapes your odds.
