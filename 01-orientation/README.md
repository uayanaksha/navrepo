# 01 — Orientation

> The first 30 minutes in a new repo shape your entire relationship with it.

Most "I can't figure this codebase out" pain comes from skipping orientation
and diving into code. This section teaches you the structured walk that
turns an unfamiliar repo into a tractable one.

- Deciding whether to even contribute to a project
